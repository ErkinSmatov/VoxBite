<objective>
Close the loop: make a typed message reach the correction it belongs to instead of the paid
pipeline (D-04), and register every Phase 4 handler in the composition root behind the allowlist
gate.

Purpose: without this, tapping `➕ Добавить` and typing "сметана" starts a brand-new paid meal
analysis of the word "сметана" (04-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 2) — the single most expensive and most
confusing failure available in this phase.
Output: the text gate inside `meal.ts`, the correction text handler, wiring, registration, and
the extended order tripwires.

DESIGN CHOICE, stated rather than assumed: the gate lives INSIDE `createTextHandler` rather than
as a second competing `bot.on('message:text', ...)` registration. grammY dispatches to
registrations in order and only continues past a handler that calls `next()`; two independent
text registrations would make the routing depend on a subtlety a future reader has to
reconstruct. One handler with a documented first gate is auditable in one place — the same
argument `meal.ts`'s existing header makes about its numbered gate order.
</objective>

| Composition-root ordering → every security property | Registration order is the enforcement point for both properties above |
